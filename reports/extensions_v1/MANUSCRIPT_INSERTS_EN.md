# Suggested manuscript inserts from the supplemental experiments

These passages describe the saved supplemental results verified on 21 October 2026. Source tables are [metrics_overall.csv](metrics_overall.csv), [paired_effects.csv](paired_effects.csv), [hpo_selected.csv](hpo_selected.csv), and [historical_diagnostics.csv](historical_diagnostics.csv); the complete Chinese report is [REPORT_ZH.md](REPORT_ZH.md). The 2015–2017 analyses use previously evaluated development origins; the 2018–2020 analyses are new-time validation within the same domain pair. They do not constitute independent-domain confirmation.

## Target-model uncertainty

Reanalysis of the original six-epoch target models found a mean Tail80 I-NDCG@20 difference of 0.003943 between the combined-history and mean-history targets, a relative point difference of 12.24%. Both the item-bootstrap interval [−0.004196, 0.011647] and the unique-item-cluster interval [−0.004455, 0.011823] crossed zero. All five prespecified target comparisons had intervals crossing zero under both procedures. These results concern 483 item–origin pairs and 480 unique items; they do not establish a consistent advantage of the combined-history target.

## Training-budget and objective-alignment controls

Across the previously evaluated 2015–2017 development origins, historical selection of configuration and training budget strengthened the collaborative baselines. Native LightGCN increased mean Tail80 I-NDCG@20 from 0.024061 to 0.034382, while SimpleX increased from 0.003425 to 0.022113; both selected 100 epochs. These comparisons jointly change the training budget and the configuration-selection procedure and therefore do not isolate an epoch-only causal effect. The results weaken conclusions based on the original six-epoch baseline ranking.

The user-retrieval objective variant of LightGCN achieved 0.042161, compared with 0.034382 for native LightGCN. The paired difference was 0.007779 (a relative point difference of 22.62%), with descriptive item and unique-item-cluster intervals of [0.001239, 0.015188] and [0.001272, 0.016124], respectively. The difference was positive at each of the three development origins. Both objectives selected 100 epochs, but their independently selected learning rates and regularization coefficients differed: 0.01 and 0.001 for the user-retrieval variant versus 0.001 and 0.00001 for native LightGCN. This comparison concerns separately tuned objectives under the same candidate grid; it does not isolate the loss at a fixed configuration.

The complete six-family search comprises 216 historical training trajectories (12 configurations and three historical seeds per family) and 90 final models (three origins and five seeds per family). Checkpoints at 6, 20 and 50 epochs are considered initially. A prespecified rule extends every configuration and seed in a family to 100 epochs only when the overall historical winner among these checkpoints is at 50 epochs and improves by at least 1% over the same configuration at 20 epochs. When the absolute 20-epoch mean is below 1e-8, an absolute improvement threshold of 1e-4 replaces the relative threshold. Five different seeds are used for each final origin. Final-query outcomes are not used to select a checkpoint or trigger extension.

## New-time validation of source correction

The new-time matrix contains 24 historical folds, 74 historical deployment-selection records, 120 policy evaluations, and three activity-baseline evaluations. Its target configurations remain frozen from the original development stage and are not replaced by Stage B selections. On the prespecified 2018–2020 music origins, full utility correction on the combined-history target achieved mean Tail80 I-NDCG@20 of 0.024223, compared with 0.024021 for the identical frozen target without correction. The paired difference was 0.000202, corresponding to a relative point estimate of 0.84%. Both the item-bootstrap interval [−0.000212, 0.000647] and the unique-item-cluster interval [−0.000192, 0.000653] crossed zero. The difference was negative in 2018 and became negative when 2019 was excluded. This comparison covers 451 item–origin pairs and 449 unique items, after averaging paired model seeds and then giving each origin equal weight. Consequently, this experiment does not establish a stable transfer benefit.

The full-utility strategy exceeded the uniform strategy by 0.000441 on average. This secondary comparison does not replace the primary comparison against the same frozen target with zero correction. The mean-history target selected a zero radius for all source policies. A globally selected zero radius does not establish that every item-specific utility prediction was nonpositive or that abstention was individually optimal.

Historical prediction diagnostics also indicate limited discrimination. For the combined-history target, sign accuracy on the three held-out historical blocks ranged from approximately 0.511 to 0.524; the 2019 selected predictor had higher mean squared error than a zero predictor. Historical loss prediction and bounded source corrections therefore provide no guarantee of subsequent ranking improvement.

## Interpretation limits

The intervals are descriptive and do not fully account for shared-user dependence. Model seeds, checkpoints and shared-policy evaluations are not independent datasets. The new-time experiments remain within the same domain pair. The resource-gated Movies and TV–to–Books extension was not run, so no independent-domain confirmation is claimed. Exact zero-correction recovery is an implementation property, and the convex loss statement applies only to its specified loss; neither guarantees an improvement in future NDCG.
